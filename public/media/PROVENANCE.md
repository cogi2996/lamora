- `lamora-coffee-hills-loop.mp4`: [Lush Green Landscape Aerial View of Farmland](https://www.pexels.com/video/lush-green-landscape-aerial-view-of-farmland-33412872/)
- `lamora-coffee-branch-loop.mp4`: [Vibrant Coffee Berries on Lush Coffee Plant](https://www.pexels.com/video/vibrant-coffee-berries-on-lush-coffee-plant-36271589/)

The matching poster PNGs are still frames extracted from each optimized MP4 and are used as the reduced-motion and loading fallback.
